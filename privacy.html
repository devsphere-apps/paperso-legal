Privacy Policy

Paperso · Effective 25 October 2026

Paperso lets you fill and sign PDF documents on your iPhone. Your documents stay on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • No account is needed, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your documents, scans, photos, signatures, initials and signer names are stored on your iPhone only.
  • The app does not upload your documents to us or to any server.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

When you use Paperso, the following is created and stored locally on your device:

  • PDF files you import, scan or create from photos.
  • Signatures and initials you draw or type, and anything you place on a page (text, dates, checkmarks, images).
  • The signer name and consent you enter when you sign, the time of signing, and the SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed file. These go into the certificate and signing record that Paperso creates on your device.

We cannot see any of this. We do not have a server that receives it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan paper, through Apple's built-in document scanner. Photos are chosen through Apple's photo picker, and the app receives only the photos you select. Metadata such as location is removed from images you place in a document.

Sharing

Paperso shares a file only when you choose to, using the iOS share sheet (for example Mail, Messages or Files). The app or person you share with handles it under their own privacy practices. Paperso does not send anything on its own.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a device backup, Apple may include the app's data in your backup according to your device settings. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting a document in Paperso removes it from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies you exported or shared stay where you saved or sent them.

Children

Paperso is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on our side. If you have a question or a request about privacy, contact us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change is released.
