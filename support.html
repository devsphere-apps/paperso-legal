Common questions

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your iPhone. Paperso has no accounts and does not upload your documents. If you delete the app, its documents are removed from the phone, so export or share anything you want to keep first.

How do I sign a PDF?

Import a PDF from Files, scan paper, or choose photos. Open it, tap Signature, draw or type your signature, place it on the page, then tap Export and choose how to share the signed file.

What is the certificate?

When you export, Paperso can create a separate certificate with the signer name, the time, the consent, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed PDF. If the file changes later, its fingerprint will no longer match.

My PDF is locked or will not open

If a PDF asks for a password, enter it when prompted. Some PDFs with strict protections cannot be signed. If a file is damaged, try opening it in another app first.

Scanning does not work

Allow camera access for Paperso in iPhone Settings, then try again.
