What Paperso does

Paperso helps you view PDFs, place fields on them, and add an electronic signature on your iPhone. It can create a signed PDF and a certificate that records the signer name, the time, your consent and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed file.

Electronic signatures and the law

  • Electronic signatures are accepted for many documents in many countries, but not all. Some documents, such as certain wills, property transfers or court filings, may need other formats or witnesses.
  • Paperso does not give legal advice. You are responsible for checking whether an electronic signature is suitable and valid for your document and where you are.
  • The signer name is typed in by the person signing. Paperso does not verify anyone's identity. The certificate and fingerprint show that the file has not been changed since it was signed. They are not proof of who signed it.
